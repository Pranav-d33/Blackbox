Blackbox Verifier

Verify a mission.

No account. No trust in the operator. Your browser reads 0G Chain and 0G Storage and re-checks every hash, signature, nonce and anchor itself.

Mission

The mission is read from BlackboxRegistry on 0G Chain. The manifest and every log entry come from 0G Storage by root hash. TEE signers are checked on 0G's InferenceServing.

Tamper-evident, not tamper-proof. A green result shows the robot acted only on answers signed by an allowlisted 0G TEE signer, each produced for its request. It does not prove what the camera saw.
