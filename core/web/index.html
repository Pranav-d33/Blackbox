The problem

Who checks the drone?

An AI-driven drone calls a solar panel clean. Months later it catches fire. Four people need to know what the drone saw and decided, and all four get the same thing: the contractor's own database.

The ownerpaid for an inspection and wants proof it happened as reported
The insureris asked to cover the loss and needs evidence it can rely on
The panel makerfaces a warranty claim and wants to know when the crack appeared
The contractorhas to defend its record without being trusted on its word

How it works

Every checkpoint, five steps.

  1. 1It holds.The camera flags a mark. The drone stops and takes a photo, fingerprinted and tied to a fresh nonce.
  2. 2It asks 0G.The photo goes to 0G's own vision model, running sealed inside a hardware enclave (TEE).
  3. 30G answers and signs."Cracked cell, severity 2, go closer", signed by the enclave's key and echoing the nonce.
  4. 4The robot checks.Signer allowlisted? Signature covers these exact bytes? Nonce echoed? Otherwise it refuses.
  5. 5It acts and records.Robot-signed, hash-chained log on 0G Storage, fingerprint anchored on 0G Chain.

No 0G, no decision. If 0G can't be reached in time, the drone flies home and the log says 0G unreachable, no decision made. It never guesses. When the link is back, it resumes from the exact point it stopped.

Try to break it

Built for the operator you can't trust.

Forge a decisionThe operator signs a fake "continue" with its own key.Refused: signer is not on the manifest allowlist
Flip 0G's answerKeep 0G's real signature, change what it said.Refused: TEE signature does not cover this answer
Edit the logLower a severity after the flight.Verifier: entry #2 was changed after the robot signed it
Cut the AIKill the link to 0G mid-flight.Safe stop: 0G unreachable, no decision made

And the counterfactual: replay the same flight with every 0G answer forced to "continue", and a second drone flies straight past the damage. 0G's answer is what moved the robot.

Why 0G

Three layers, all in the control loop.

0G ComputeSealed inference on 0G's own vision model. The TEE signature is what the robot checks before it moves.
0G StorageThe manifest, every robot-signed log entry and the evidence, by content root. Camera crops are encrypted.
0G ChainBlackboxRegistry: mission registered before takeoff, anchored every two decisions, closed with the trajectory hash.

Plus Arkiv as the query layer: every finding becomes a row you can search by panel, maker or batch, owned by the wallet that wrote it. Arkiv makes it searchable; 0G decides whether it's true.

Why web3

The one who runs the drone is the one who could edit the record.

Owner, insurer, panel maker and contractor don't trust each other. A signed log alone proves the file wasn't edited. It can't prove which model decided, that the answer was made for this checkpoint, or that the robot did only what it was told. A public chain gives all four a commitment none of them can quietly rewrite, checkable from a phone with no account.

Why Arkiv, when we have 0G

0G proves it. Arkiv makes it askable.

0G Storage returns a file by its content root, and nothing else. Nobody at a solar farm asks for a root. They ask for module r2m13's history, which panels are still cracked, and whether cracks cluster in one maker's batch: a warranty claim.

QueriesEvery finding is an Arkiv entity, filterable by panel, label, severity, maker and batch, straight from the browser.
OwnershipThe site owner's wallet owns the asset register, the inspector's wallet owns the findings. No admin who can rewrite both.
Expiry + backupThe working set expires after 90 days; the proof on 0G stays. If 0G Storage can't serve a blob, the verifier reads Arkiv's mirror and re-checks it against 0G Chain.

Arkiv is never trusted for the verdict, only for the question. Swap it for Postgres and the operator owns the database again.

SDK

Any robot. Three calls.

Blackbox is a recorder SDK, not a one-off demo. Implement Robot, write a mission profile (one JSON file: what the AI may answer, what the robot may do), and every decision is sealed, signed and logged on 0G. Solar farms and bridge decks are two profiles on the same code. A pipeline weld or a turbine blade is another file.

The input doesn't have to be a camera. A typed note or a spoken command from the crew goes through the same sealed model and the same interlock.
